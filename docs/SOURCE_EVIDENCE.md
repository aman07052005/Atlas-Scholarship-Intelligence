## Evidence interpretation
A scheme's presence on NSP is strong evidence that the named scheme is listed for the AY and that the displayed portal dates apply. It is not evidence for every eligibility or award field. Those fields are left unspecified unless the provider's own scheme page or guideline supports them.

## Amount-evidence cross-checks used by the verification gate

The amount evidence excerpts in `data/field_evidence.csv` are paired with these official primary-source pages/documents. The captured excerpt records the amount text (or qualitative benefit components) rather than merely saying that an amount exists:

- UGC Ishan Uday: https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship5 — financial assistance Rs. 80,000 per annum.
- UGC National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies: https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship6 — financial assistance Rs. 1,50,000 per annum.
- UGC P.G. Scholarship for University Rank Holders: https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship1 — Rs. 37,200 per annum.
- UGC P.G. Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child: https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship4 — Rs. 36,200 per annum.
- ICAR NTS guideline: https://www.icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/Guidelines%20for%20ICAR%20NTS%20Scholarship.pdf — Rs. 2,000 per month for undergraduate students and Rs. 3,000 per month for postgraduate students.
- MHA PMSS revised guideline: https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/PMSSrevisedguidelines_23112020.pdf — Rs. 3,000 per month for girls and Rs. 2,500 per month for boys; also extends the scheme to eligible State Police wards from AY 2019-20.
- Ministry of Tribal Affairs scholarship page: https://tribal.nic.in/Scholarship.aspx — fellowship rates of Rs. 25,000 per month for M.Phil and Rs. 28,000 per month for Ph.D; the separate Top Class ST scholarship benefit is described as tuition, admission/non-refundable fees, stipend and book/computer allowances.

Historical/current-cycle distinctions remain explicit. A source that establishes scheme identity or a historical rate does not automatically establish that the current application window is open. The crawler stores the current-cycle evidence separately and applies the 180-day freshness gate.
